Who I am

I’m a Mechanical & Automation Engineering student, with a calm demeanour yet bold personality, drawn to the way physics turns into motion, mechanisms turn into machines, and ideas turn into systems

I’m curious about both the foundations and the possibilities they lead to — from understanding how forces, materials, energy, and motion behave to exploring automation, robotics, and intelligent systems. I like learning by making things, testing them, and occasionally figuring out why they didn’t work.
